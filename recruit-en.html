CAREERS AT SICHAE

Help build what’s next
in automation control.

SICHAE is a startup developing an automation control software platform, drawing on hands-on experience in semiconductor, solar, and battery manufacturing. Join us in turning real factory-floor challenges into better software.

AREAS OF INTEREST

We’re interested in people across these disciplines.

We are open to conversations across a range of roles. We’ll discuss fit based on your experience and SICHAE’s development priorities.

01 · CONTROL

Automation control software

People interested in or experienced with PC and PLC control, equipment sequences, interlocks, and device communication.

02 · MOTION & SYSTEMS

Motion, electrical, and system integration

People comfortable connecting motion control, I/O, sensors, actuators, and equipment configurations.

03 · VISION & DATA

Vision, image processing, and MES

People interested in industrial cameras, image detection, equipment data, and MES integration.

Specific roles and timing will depend on development plans and candidate experience.

HOW WE WORK

The kind of teammates we value.

We value people who work through real-world problems with curiosity and care, alongside their technical skills.

Curious about real equipment challenges

You look beyond symptoms to understand causes and how the system works.

Enjoy learning and trying new approaches

You are motivated to learn control technologies and software, and make steady improvements.

Communicate technical work clearly

You share progress and engineering decisions in a way teammates and customers can understand.

Adapt well in an early-stage team

You take initiative and find a path forward as priorities evolve.

Work well with a remote team

We work primarily from home and meet by video twice a week to share progress and align on next steps.
