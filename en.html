BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Bringing fragmented controls
together in software.

SICHAE is building on automation control experience to develop a platform for addressing recurring field problems more consistently.

PROBLEM

Control development often starts over for every machine.

Control architectures, device interfaces, and process conditions vary by site and machine, so similar issues often require fresh analysis and custom work. Sequence faults and signal-integration issues can be difficult to diagnose, while control functions are spread across PC, PLC, vision, and MES systems.

SOLUTION DIRECTION

Developing a field-adaptable automation control platform.

The platform is being designed around SICHAE’s capabilities in PC and PLC control, motion, vision, electrical systems, and MES integration. The development goal is to structure common control elements while allowing flexibility for customer-specific equipment and processes.

The problem definition and solution direction reflect the current business concept. Platform functions and scope will be refined through development and field validation.

  • Connecting recognition results to control flows
